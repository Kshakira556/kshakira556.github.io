About Me

Hi, I'm Shakira Knight, a Business Systems & Operations Analyst with experience in business analysis, process improvement, business intelligence, and full-stack software development.

My career began in operations, enterprise sales, and customer success, where I managed national client portfolios, improved operational performance, and delivered technology solutions for organisations including MTN Business, Halo Mobility, and Digital District.

Today I combine business knowledge with technical skills to transform business problems into practical, data-driven solutions. My work includes business process analysis, SQL, Excel, Power BI, dashboard development, workflow automation, API integrations, and modern web applications.

My portfolio includes end-to-end analytics projects covering business understanding, data preparation, SQL analysis, interactive dashboards, customer segmentation, executive reporting, and strategic recommendations.

Outside of work I enjoy weightlifting, chess, psychology, and learning new technologies that help organisations make better decisions through data.
